# Historical qualification — 2026-10-12

This is a curated record of a frozen **pre-cleanup P0** on macOS/Apple Silicon with Python 3.14.4. It describes that tested source, not the subsequently cleaned 0.1.0a1 release. Original private reports, run states, traces, and the full source manifest remain separate; no credentials, captured payloads, provider response bodies, or raw reasoning are published here. [Aggregate evidence](2026-10-12.json) retains report hashes and reproducibility identifiers.

## What was tested

The runtime used CPU Laya with four threads and revision `e929ae5cf69bc34259cd2f95c9e91145b818b1f0`, DREX `nace-ai/drex-v1.5`, GPT-4.1-mini reducers, and GPT-5.1 System 2. Ten independent workflows were frozen before execution; exploratory preparation runs are excluded from the denominator.

Laya micro precision was .880597, recall was .95 for conversation and 1.0 for telemetry/video, and no-destination specificity was .75. Original routing-quality targets were unmet; a prototype-specific owner exception accepted this error rate. It did **not** waive CPU performance or factual-answer quality. The checkpoint also clamped an invalid temperature to .5, so affected confidence was uncalibrated.

Across the ten workflows, 1,099 native predictions had mean 1.392559 seconds and p95 2.113812 seconds. Run 05 timed out at 5.002132 seconds and stopped further pops as intended. Its 54 queued signals were not replayed. Paced workflow inputs and the simultaneous burst are separate workloads.

## Thinking controls and factual failures

One direct System 2 sample at each thinking level used the same immutable synthetic context:

| Level | Provider effort | Latency | Reported reasoning tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| `none` | `none` | 1.861935 seconds | 0 |
| `some` | `low` | 3.505628 seconds | 104 |
| `extra` | `high` | 9.753408 seconds | 977 |

Each made one attempt, excluded reasoning output, and omitted completion-budget and tool fields. These three samples verify controls and those answers; they do not establish a general advantage from additional thinking.

Runs 03, 08, and 10 reported source `error_rate=0.45` as `0.45%`, although the declared fraction means **45%**. Reducer summaries introduced the error and System 2 repeated it. Typed facts and citations stayed correct, but existing lifecycle/citation checks did not catch the numerical conflict. Minor unsupported trend wording was recorded separately. Explicit fractional units and authoritative typed-fact precedence were proposed for subsequent source; they were not applied during this frozen batch.

**Overall P0 qualification remained unmet.** Runtime-only success does not clear factual quality, incomplete coverage is not zero-violation proof for every run, and suppression on 50 survivors is not lossless processing of 100 admissions. Provider-reported subtotals also omit some cancelled-call charges and are not account billing totals.
